# Structure Blocks Tutorial

Hello. You're about to learn how to use a structure block to:

- Rotate a structure
- Take a structure without the mobs
- Take just the mobs out of a structure (think: chicken replicator)
- Take a section of the world and use it like an "x-ray"
- Use redstone to activate a structure block and load in a saved selection (hint: Press button, get chickens)
- Duplicate a redstone contraption to you can try variants of it to adjust timing and other tricky bits
